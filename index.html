    About this portal

    A free, open-source place to practise French, built around spaced repetition. Study immediately — there is no account, nothing to pay, and no advertising.

    Your data stays on your device

    There is no server and no database. Everything you study is stored in this browser only. Nothing about your learning is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.
