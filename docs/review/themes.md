## Things the drafter was unsure about

- Levels are rough estimates throughout; borderline: portable, coût, remboursement, réchauffement, internaute.
- « portable »: masculine for the phone; the gloss « mobile phone; laptop » is ambiguous in current French.
- Persian glosses with a weak fit: rayon (بخش), tableau (تابلو), chronique (ستون نظر), dépaysement (احساس غریبگی دلپذیر), épreuve (آزمون), correspondance (تعویض خط), mémoire as dissertation (پایان‌نامه).
- One sense kept for ambiguous nouns: devoir (homework in education, duty in society), poste (post office only), société (company, in work).
- Persian: پیراهن used for both shirt and dress; check اسراف for gaspillage, and the loanwords ریتم and استرس.
- Formal-letter phrases (« Je me permets de contester… », « Je vous serais reconnaissant… ») and France-specific facts (president elected every five years, vote at 18, Châtelet and line 4).
- Multi-word nouns were left out by rule (salle de bains, carte bancaire, mot de passe, titre de séjour, pièce d'identité, réseau social…); several are exam-essential and could become expressions.
